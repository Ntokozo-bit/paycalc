Settings

Rates & Overtime

Normal Paid Hours sets both the daily overtime point and the ordinary paid hours used for a scheduled public holiday. Normal-day overtime is usually 1.5×; it is never stacked onto public-holiday pay.

A Sunday in your Week Template is treated as usual work (normally 1.5×); other Sundays normally use 2×. Public-holiday work uses the BCEA daily-wage formula with a 2× minimum.

How to use

Start here

Three steps, then you are ready

  1. Set your pay.Open Settings, add your hourly rate, Normal Paid Hours, pay-cycle start day, and Week Template.
  2. Add or check a day.Tap its calendar date. For a normal shift, use Save Normal Day. For different hours, enter the real start, finish, and break.
  3. Read the result.The selected-day card shows the hours, pay, and rule. Cycle is the full estimate; Basic Salary excludes premium pay.

Public holidays

WorkPay detects South African public holidays automatically. If the holiday is normally a workday, it is saved with your Normal Paid Hours even when you do not work.

Only turn on I worked this public holiday when you actually worked, then enter your start, finish, and break. Holiday work uses the BCEA daily-wage formula and does not add normal 1.5× overtime.

Correct old data

Use Prev Month, Next Month, or the month box. Tap an old saved day—or its Edit button—change the details, and save. The totals update immediately.

Keep your data safe

Settings → Data & Device lets you download a restorable backup and install WorkPay. The app works offline after its first successful load. CSV is for Excel or Sheets; it is not a backup.

Your data stays in this browser. Automatically detected holidays cover the statutory calendar and Sunday-to-Monday observance; manually declared one-off holidays can still be marked. Contracts, bargaining councils and sector rules may provide better terms.
